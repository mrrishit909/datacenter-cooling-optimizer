Data Center Cooling Optimizer

An invented hall of 48 racks and four CRAC units. A thermal model fitted to sixty days of its sensors, 24 GPU training servers placed two ways, set-points raised as far as the model allows, and a CRAC failure handled by reading the sensors rather than trusting the model, every step scored in the simulated hall. …
The hall, its loads, sensors, CRACs, prices and the physics that moves heat through it are generated by a lumped simulator in this repository, not CFD and not a real data center. Its true temperatures are shown only to judge the models, which see nothing but the sensor readings. No outside model or service is called.
